The speech is a made-up cooking video with Chinese translations. Its timings follow the live server's: the first update about 0.9 s after a word is spoken, an update every 0.5 s, a line finished 0.85 s after the speaker stops, and its translation 0.45 s later. A few words are misheard at first and corrected, as the recogniser does. The live translation arrives 0.35 s after each update; here it is approximated by showing the finished translation in proportion to the words heard, so a real one will reorder words more as the sentence grows.
