# Real captions

One caption clause per line, per caption language (`<code>.txt`): what
the live server's speech model wrote, in Polyglotic's own validation runs
(polyglotic-hq 346's loop), while public recordings played. Each distinct
clause is here once.

`tools.dictionaries.cantonese.said` counts the words of `yue.txt` with
HKCanCor's, as what Cantonese speech says (polyglotic-hq 467): a word
the captions write isn't ranked as written only, however rarely HKCanCor's
conversations of 1997-98 say it (被, 從, 時 and 以及 are news Cantonese).
Only counts made from it reach a pack.

`yue.txt`: 152 clauses, from runs of 2026-11-15 to 2026-11-17 (projects
371 and 450) on two recordings (the sources as project 371 recorded them,
`projects/371-a-better-cantonese-experience/audio/SOURCES.md`):

- "美國陪審團的否決權 VOA.mp3": a Voice of America Cantonese report on
  jury nullification. Public domain (a US government work, as Wikimedia
  Commons states).
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%E7%BE%8E%E5%9C%8B%E9%99%AA%E5%AF%A9%E5%9C%98%E7%9A%84%E5%90%A6%E6%B1%BA%E6%AC%8A_VOA.mp3
- "Zh-yue-Steve Jobs.oga": the Cantonese Wikipedia's article on Steve
  Jobs read aloud. Public domain, as Wikimedia Commons states.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Zh-yue-Steve_Jobs.oga

Both are only a few minutes of two topics, so they don't rank words of
their own: they only keep a word that real captions write from counting
as written only.
