# Our Hindi words

The 0.1.12 catalog carries forward the Hindi reviewed in project 878 on
2026-11-24. The refresh reviews new and revised English. The
[native review list](review/review-hi.md) records terms to try in the app.

Use everyday Hindi, familiar software loanwords, and polite commands like
चुनें and करें. Use Western digits and keep Polyglotic, Plus, Anki, Windows,
HSK and keyboard names in Latin script. Hindi uses Nirmala UI.
