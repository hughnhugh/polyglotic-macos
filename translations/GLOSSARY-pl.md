# Our Polish words

The app's own terms in Polish, said one way in every string. Reviewed for natural, idiomatic Polish following **Windows PL**, **macOS PL**, **OBS Studio PL**, **Discord PL**, and **Anki PL** conventions.

Style rules:
- Friendly **„ty”** form, consistent with Polish Windows 11, Discord, and Anki.
- **Lowercase** `twój` / `cię` / `ciebie` / `tobą` mid-sentence (standard Polish UI style; capitalized only at the start of a sentence).
- **Gender-neutral Polish** — avoid masculine-only past tense (`usłyszałeś`, `zgubiłeś`, `postawiłem`) by using present tense, impersonal/passive forms, or noun phrases.
- Polish typography: `„…”` quotation marks, spaced en-dash ` – `, single ellipsis `…`, no space before `%` (`70%`), space before units (`2 s`, `12 px`, `5 MB`).
- Counts in three Polish plural forms (1 `napis`, 2–4 `napisy`, 0/5+ `napisów`), with grammatical case matching the governing preposition or verb (e.g. `od %Ln sekundy` / `od %Ln sekund`).
- Never decline `{language}` / `{languages}` after a preposition (`w`, `z`, `dla`, `na`, `o`, `od`, etc.) — use nominative apposition, colon, or parentheses so automatic sentence substitution stays grammatical.
- Keep `PC` (`komputer`) and `Mac` (`Mac`, declined naturally: `Maca`, `na tym Macu`, `odtwarza Mac`) strictly distinct in every `pc_or_mac` pair.
- Product & technical names that stay unchanged: Polyglotic, Plus, Lite, Standard, Premium, Anki, Windows, macOS, Mac, HSK, Pinyin, Jyutping, Zhuyin, Furigana.
