# Our Japanese words

The app's own terms in Japanese, said one way in every string. **A machine
draft** (polyglotic-hq 294, 2026-11-13, by Claude) for a native speaker's
review: correct a term here first, then every string using it.

How the Japanese is written: です・ます in sentences, short plain forms on
buttons; full-width punctuation (、。：？！「」); a half-width space only
around Latin words and numbers where Windows' own Japanese does; drawn in
Yu Gothic UI or Meiryo UI, never a Chinese face (`style.SCRIPT_FACES`);
Polyglotic, Plus, Anki, Windows, HSK stay as they are.
