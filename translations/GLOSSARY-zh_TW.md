# Our Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) words

The interface in Traditional Chinese, as Taiwan writes it - for Taiwan,
Hong Kong and Macau Windows. First drafted in project 156 (2026-11-07)
from the Simplified through OpenCC's `s2twp`; **rewritten by hand in
polyglotic-hq 842 (2026-11-24)** with the Simplified (`GLOSSARY-zh_CN.md`,
its "How the Chinese is written" and its categorical fixes apply here
too), in Taiwan's words and Taiwan's quotation marks 「」. Machine
conversion left mainland phrasing in it; a new string is written by hand
with these words, not converted.

The models: Windows 11's Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), OBS Studio's
(音訊輸出擷取, 視窗擷取), Apple's Taiwan Chinese (自動續訂), LINE.
