# Our Filipino words

The app's own terms in Filipino, said one way in every string. Project 877
reviewed the draft on 2026-11-24 and refreshed it for 0.1.12 on 2026-11-26.
The review sheet records wording to check with a native Filipino speaker.

Use friendly Filipino (“mo”, “ka”). Keep familiar software words such as
app, PC, caption, server, deck and hotkey when they read naturally. Keep
Polyglotic, Plus, Anki, Windows and HSK.
