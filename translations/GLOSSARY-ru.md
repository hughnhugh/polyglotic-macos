# Our Russian words

The app's own terms in Russian, said one way in every string. The machine
draft (Polyglotic HQ 294, 2026-11-13) was reviewed for meaning and fit on
2026-11-24 and refreshed for 0.1.12. The review sheet lists 20 entries for a
native speaker. Update the glossary and the affected strings together when a
term changes.

How the Russian is written: "вы" (lower case), as Russian software does;
«ёлочки» for quotes; counts in three forms (1 субтитр, 2 субтитра, 5
субтитров); short forms where a button has little room; Polyglotic, Plus,
Anki, Windows, HSK stay as they are.
