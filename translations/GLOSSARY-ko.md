# Our Korean words

The app's own terms in Korean, said one way in every string. Reviewed 2026-11-24 (polyglotic-hq 851 and 861) and refreshed for 0.1.12 on
2026-11-25 (polyglotic-hq 971). See [20 choices for native review](review/review-ko.md).

How the Korean is written:
- Polite 합니다/하세요 in full sentences and explanations; concise verb or
  action noun forms on buttons (e.g. 캡처, 저장, 보기, 초기화, 다시 시작).
- Every Korean language name ends in a vowel syllable (`영어`, `중국어(번체)`),
  so particles after `{language}` / `{languages}` use `를`, `가`, `는`, `로`
  (never `을(를)`, `이(가)`, `은(는)`, `(으)로` — checked by test).
- Natural Korean punctuation and spacing; replace English ` - ` with `: `,
  `, `, or a sentence break where clearer.
- Drawn in Malgun Gothic (`style.SCRIPT_FACES`).
- Product and standard names stay as they are: Polyglotic, Plus, Lite, Anki,
  Windows, Mac, Discord, Zoom, Chrome, HSK, SRT, CSV.
