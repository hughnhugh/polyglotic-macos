# Our French words

The app's own terms in French, said one way in every string. **Reviewed and
standardized** (project 852, 2026-11-24) for a native speaker's final pass:
correct a term here first, then every string using it.

How the French is written:
- Strictly **"vous"** throughout (never "tu"/"ton" or 2sg imperatives); infinitive verbs on action buttons, as standard in French Windows/macOS software.
- Proper French typography: a plain space before `: ; ! ?` and `%` (a reviewer may turn them into non-breaking spaces); straight apostrophe `'` everywhere for uniformity; `« guillemets »`; avoid bare English-style ` - ` dashes in prose (prefer `:`, `;`, `,`, or `—`).
- Brand and proper names stay as they are: Polyglotic, Plus, Lite, Standard, Premium, Anki, Windows, macOS, HSK, Pinyin, Zhuyin, Jyutping, Furigana.
- Language names in sentences: never place `le`, `la`, `de`, `du`, or `que` directly before `{language}` / `{languages}` (elision safety for vowel-initial names like *anglais*, *arabe*). Use `en {language}`, `dictionnaire {language}`, `cette langue ({language})`, or `{language} : …`.
