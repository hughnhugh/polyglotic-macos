# Our Spanish words

The app's own terms in Spanish, said one way in every string. **Critically
audited and revised** (polyglotic-hq 852, 2026-11-24) for a native speaker's
review: correct a term here first, then every string using it.

How the Spanish is written: neutral Spanish for Spain and Latin America
(avoiding regionalisms such as *ordenador*, *vídeo* or *hora punta*);
"tú" register, friendly and concise like the English; ¿…? and ¡…!; single
ellipsis `…` (never `...`); space before `%` (`50 %`, per RAE); no English
`" - "` hyphen-dashes (use `:`, `;`, `,` or parentheses); sentence case in
buttons (infinitive for actions/menus/toggles, imperative for direct user
prompts); Polyglotic, Plus, Lite, Anki, Windows, macOS, Mac, HSK, Pinyin,
Zhuyin, Jyutping and Furigana stay as they are.
