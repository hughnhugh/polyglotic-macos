# Our Dutch words

The app's own terms in Dutch, said one way in every string. The 2026-11-24 review used terminology from **Windows (nl-NL)**, **macOS (nl-NL)**, **OBS Studio (nl)**, **Discord (nl)**, and **Anki (nl)** conventions.

How the Dutch is written: friendly "je"/"jij" everywhere (never "u"/"uw", except the hour abbreviation `{hours} u`); 'enkele aanhalingstekens'; Dutch compound words written together (`ondertitelvenster`, `pc-geluid`, `systeemvakmenu`, `app-logboek`); spaced en-dash ` – ` for parenthetical breaks; single ellipsis character `…`; infinitives on action buttons (`Vastleggen`, `Opslaan`, `Bekijken`); Polyglotic, Plus, Lite, Premium, Anki, Windows, macOS, Linux, HSK stay as they are.
