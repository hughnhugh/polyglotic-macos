# Polish interface review for 0.1.12

All 2,183 entries have Polish text. The refresh kept 1,819 reviewed translations in place and reused 33 with identical English in a new context. It translated 289 revised entries and 42 additions. Those counts follow the English source changes and the controls they replace.

| Learning, presets, lookups, Words and saved sentences | 89 |
| Home, Help, sound and caption setup | 57 |

| App and tray actions, Settings, hotkeys, copying and feedback | 58 |

Across the revised and new entries, four troubleshooting strings use `wyciszony` for actual audio mute, and Home uses `brak dźwięku` for an idle app. Three mic notices explain when audio is sent. Seven pace labels and announcements show compact figures and spell out the unit in accessibility announcements, and three Long lines entries distinguish row-by-row translation from grouped text. The 12 entries in the pronunciation controls cover readings and the Japanese dictionary state.

The earlier pass uses `Napisy` and `Napisy dla {app}` for Home’s capture controls and `Przedstawianie słów` for the two Word introductions headings. For a later native review, compare those four inherited labels against the glossary’s `Przechwytuj`, `Przechwytuj: {app}` and `Wyróżnianie słów`.
