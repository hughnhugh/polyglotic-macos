# German wording for native review

These are the 20 wording choices I would most like a native German reader to
check in 0.1.12. The German column shows the wording in the catalog.
The preset label „Blass voraus“ and the count „{n}× seit Aufnahme in den
Stapel“ are marked unfinished. Check what the preview label conveys and
whether the count reads as times heard since the word entered the deck.
