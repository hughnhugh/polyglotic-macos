# Our Turkish words

Turkish product terms from the critical review for project 872, refreshed for 0.1.12. The [native review sheet](review/review-tr.md) lists wording to try in the interface. Agree on a term here before updating the strings that use it.

Use the friendly singular “sen” in new copy. Use “tırnak” for quotes, and keep names such as Polyglotic, Plus, Anki, Windows and HSK. Separate suffixes on proper names with an apostrophe (Polyglotic'i).
