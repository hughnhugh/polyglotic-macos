# Our German words

The app's own terms in German, said one way in every string. Reviewed and
aligned with standard German desktop conventions: **Windows German**
(*Infobereich*, *Schaltflächen*, *Einstellungen*, *Task-Manager*), **macOS
German** (*Systemeinstellungen*, *Menüleiste*, *Datenschutz & Sicherheit*,
*Programme*), **OBS Studio German** (*Erfassen*, *Quelle*, *Voreinstellung*),
**Discord German** (friendly *du*, *Tastenkürzel*, *stumm*), and **Anki
German** (*Stapel*, *Notiztyp*, *Schlagwörter*).

How the German is written: friendly „du“ throughout (never formal „Sie“);
standard full imperatives (*Klicke*, *Wähle*, *Öffne*, *Prüfe*, *Schalte*,
*Ziehe*, *Drücke* – never clipped colloquial forms); German quotes „…“;
en-dash „–“; single ellipsis character „…“ without preceding space; space
before „%“ (*91 %*); short forms where a button has little room; *Polyglotic*,
*Plus*, *Lite*, *Standard*, *Premium*, *Anki*, *Windows*, *macOS*, *HSK*,
*Pinyin*, *Zhuyin*, *Jyutping*, and *Furigana* stay as they are.
